democrac.ie is a simple way to make a decision together with your friends, family, or colleagues. It allows you to create a quick list of options, share it with others, and have them vote on their preferred choice.

# USP

Polls use PR-STV (not to the legal letter of the Irish consitution)
But it is basically instant runoff
everyone votes 1,2,3, etc in order of preference.
You do not have to rank all options, you can just rank your top 3 or 5, etc.
Also known as "instant runoff voting" or "ranked choice voting", this method ensures that the winning option has broad support, rather than just a simple majority.

Key features:
- **EVERY VOTE COUNTS**: if your top choice sores low, that option is eliminated, and your vote is transferred to your next preferred option. This process continues until one option has a majority of votes.
